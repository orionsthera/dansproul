A Simple Way To Choose

Match the gift to the connection.

If you are unsure where to begin, think about what the recipient already talks about, travels to or hangs on their walls.

The Traveler

Choose a place they have visited, lived near or keep talking about returning to.

The Wildlife Watcher

Start with their favorite animal — moose, eagle, bear, owl or another species they always notice.

The Home Decor Person

Look for calming water, warm forest light, mountain scale or monochrome work that fits how they already decorate.

The Hard-To-Buy-For Person

Use a smaller print, poster, puzzle or useful product tied to a place or subject they already care about.

Nature Photography Gifts & Travel Art

Meaningful gifts built around places, wildlife and memory.

A nature photography gift can be especially personal when the image connects to something the recipient already loves. Instead of choosing generic wall decor, shoppers can begin with a favorite national park, wildlife subject, coastline, mountain region or hometown.
