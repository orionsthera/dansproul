About the Work

Original artwork built for
professional interiors

Dan Sproul creates original fine art photography and mixed media artwork for hospitality, healthcare, corporate, and residential interiors across the United States. From the misty cliffs of the Oregon Coast to the glacier lakes of Alaska, each image is crafted with the final print in mind — sharp, rich, and designed to hold at large format.

Every file is print-optimized at full resolution with careful attention to tonal range, shadow detail, and color accuracy across canvas, metal, acrylic, and fine art paper. The result is wall art that looks as intentional at 60 inches as it does at 20.

Dan works directly with designers, procurement teams, and project managers to source the right images for each space — whether that's a single statement piece or a curated collection across an entire floor or facility.

Print Formats

Every Format, Any Scale

All prints are produced on professional-grade materials through certified fine art labs. Custom sizing available on all formats.

01

Canvas Prints

Gallery-wrapped on 1.5" or 2" solid wood stretcher bars using archival canvas and UV-resistant inks. Ready to hang. Available as single panels or multi-panel split installations up to 120" wide.

Standard sizes: 12×18 to 60×90

Custom sizing available

75-year archival rating

02

Metal Prints

Dye-sublimation infused directly into aluminum sheet. Produces vivid, luminous color with exceptional depth and a semi-gloss or matte finish. Ideal for high-traffic or high-humidity environments including spas, restaurants, and lobbies.

Standard sizes: 8×10 to 48×72

Flush mount or float mount

Waterproof · Scratch resistant

03

Framed Prints

Archival fine art paper prints with acid-free mat and professional framing. Available in black, white, natural wood, and silver. Custom mat sizing and double-mat options. Ideal for healthcare environments where a traditional, approachable aesthetic is preferred.

Frame finishes: 4 options

Mat: single or double

Museum glass available

04

Acrylic Face Mounts

Print face-mounted behind 1/4" optical-quality acrylic. Creates a floating, gallery-depth appearance with vivid color. Particularly effective for large statement pieces in hotel lobbies, executive suites, and architectural interiors.

Standard sizes: 16×24 to 48×72

Standoff or flush wall mount

High gloss finish

05

Fine Art Paper

Giclée prints on 100% cotton rag or baryta paper using 12-color archival ink sets. For designers who prefer to frame locally or through their own vendors. Printed and shipped flat or rolled in archival tubes.

Papers: cotton rag, baryta, lustre

Ships flat or rolled

100+ year archival rating

06

Digital Licensing

Purchase high-resolution digital files and print through your preferred local or commercial lab. Full-resolution TIFF or JPEG files delivered digitally within 24 hours. Single-use and multi-use commercial licenses available.

Files: TIFF / JPEG / sRGB or AdobeRGB
