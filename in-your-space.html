Print Samples & Media Options

Every print, every medium

Dan's photography is available in four premium print media. Each produces a completely different look and feel in your space. Browse the samples below — or contact Dan to request a custom quote for your home, office, or hospitality project.

Framed Prints

Archival paper · Hand-crafted frames

Museum-quality archival paper in a range of frame styles — white, silver, dark wood, and black float. Includes UV-protective glass. Ready to hang.

Browse Framed Prints ↗

Canvas Prints

Gallery-wrapped · Ready to hang

Premium cotton canvas, gallery-wrapped and kiln-dried pine stretcher bars. No frame needed — arrives ready to hang. Durable, lightweight, and stunning at large sizes.

Browse Canvas Prints ↗

Metal Prints

Dye-infused aluminum · Vivid & bold

Inks are infused directly into aluminum — no paper, no glass. The result is unmatched color vibrancy and depth. Waterproof, scratch-resistant, and perfect for modern spaces.

Browse Metal Prints ↗

Acrylic Prints

Face-mounted · Crystal clarity

Your image is face-mounted under 1/4" acrylic — the result is extraordinary depth, luminosity, and color accuracy. Premium float mount included. A gallery-level statement.
