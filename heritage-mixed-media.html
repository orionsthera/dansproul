Practical Buying Help

How to use heritage mixed media artwork in a room.

These pieces work best when the artwork helps explain the location, story, or atmosphere of a space instead of acting like generic wall filler.

Best forRestaurants, boutique hotels, Airbnbs, lodge lounges, farmhouse interiors, offices, studies, corridors, and regional commercial spaces.
Suggested sizes24×36 for smaller walls, 30×40 for entries and offices, 40×60 or larger for hospitality, restaurants, lounges, and statement walls.
Strongest formatsFramed paper for refined interiors, canvas for warmth, acrylic for polished statement spaces, and metal for high-traffic commercial walls.

FAQ

Heritage & mixed media artwork questions.

Can these pieces be ordered in custom sizes?

Yes. Most artwork can be ordered in standard or custom sizes depending on the wall, room scale, and final print format.

Do you provide mockups for designers or businesses?

Yes. Send a room photo or project direction and Dan can recommend artwork and provide mockup guidance for placement and scale.

What print materials work best for mixed media artwork?

Framed paper gives a refined gallery feel, canvas adds warmth, acrylic creates a polished statement, and metal can work well in busy commercial spaces.

Is this page mainly for designers or individual buyers?

Both. The structure is designer-friendly, but individual buyers can use the same guidance to choose artwork for homes, offices, rentals, and hospitality spaces.
