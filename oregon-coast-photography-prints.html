Wild coast, unforgettable light.

The Oregon Coast runs for 363 miles of state-protected shoreline. In a single day you can watch a fiery red sunset behind Bandon's gothic sea stacks, stand inside the lens room of Heceta Head lighthouse, and walk a foggy beach at Cannon Beach alone except for the puffins. Dan has photographed this coast extensively over multiple trips, returning in every season to chase the light.

These prints represent years of early mornings, wet gear, and patient waiting. They bring the raw power and quiet beauty of the Pacific Northwest into your home, office, or hotel — in any size, on any medium.

"The Oregon Coast never gives you the same photograph twice. Every tide, every wave breaking on the same rock is different. That's why I keep going back."

Dan Sproul, Lima, Ohio — sproulphoto@gmail.com

Oregon Coast Prints — Common Questions

Where were these Oregon photographs taken?

Dan has photographed extensively along the entire Oregon Coast — Cannon Beach and Ecola State Park in the north, Lincoln City and Yachats along the central coast, the Heceta Head and Yaquina Head lighthouses near Florence and Newport, Cape Perpetua's dramatic overlook, and Bandon Beach's iconic sea stacks in the south. He makes multiple trips per year to catch different light and weather.

What sizes are Oregon Coast prints available in?

All Oregon Coast images are available from 8x10 up to 40x60 inches and larger on canvas and metal. The Cannon Beach panoramas and Ecola State Park images are especially striking at large format — 36x24 and above. Custom sizes are available. Email sproulphoto@gmail.com for a direct quote.

Do Oregon prints ship worldwide?

Yes — prints ship to all 50 US states and over 21 countries. Orders are fulfilled through Dan's partner print lab at dansproul.com with full satisfaction guarantee.

Are Oregon prints available for commercial projects?

Absolutely. Dan works with interior designers, hospitality groups, and commercial clients. Recent projects include Drury Inn hotels and the Wexner Medical Center at Ohio State University. Email sproulphoto@gmail.com for trade pricing and bulk quotes.

Can I order a custom size not listed online?

Yes. Email sproulphoto@gmail.com with the image name, size, and medium you want and Dan will provide a direct quote. Large-format prints up to 60 inches wide are available, and split-panel triptych arrangements can be created for any of the wider images.
