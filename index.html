About Dan

A deep connection to
nature & the outdoors.

Dan Sproul is an award-winning fine art photographer and artist based in Lima, Ohio. Driven by a lifelong passion for nature and the art of capturing it, Dan spends countless hours hiking through some of America's most extraordinary wild places — always with deep respect for wildlife and the environments he photographs.

Shooting with a Canon 5D Mark IV, 90D, and 7D paired with a versatile range of lenses, Dan's work spans vivid landscape photography, intimate wildlife portraits, serene waterfall studies, and unique mixed media creations — including vintage patent drawings, historic maps, and textured city composites. Each image reflects a genuine personal connection to the subject.

His commercial work has taken him across the country, including a recent large-scale installation at the new Wexner Medical Center at Ohio State University in Columbus and an ongoing relationship with Drury Inn hotels. His prints have been placed in homes, boutique hotels, medical centers, and corporate offices across all 50 states and 21 countries.
