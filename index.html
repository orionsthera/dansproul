About Dan

A deep connection to
nature & the outdoors.

Dan Sproul is an award-winning fine art photographer and artist based in Lima, Ohio. With over a decade behind the lens, his work captures breathtaking beauty from America's most iconic wild places — the glacial lakes of Alaska, the dramatic Oregon coastline, the blazing autumn aspens of Colorado, and the wildlife-rich coves of the Great Smoky Mountains.

His diverse portfolio spans vivid landscape photography, dramatic wildlife portraits, serene waterfall studies, and unique mixed media creations — including vintage patent drawings, historic maps, and textured composites. Each piece is crafted to bring the wonder of the natural world into your home or office.

Dan's prints have sold to thousands of collectors, businesses, and hotel chains across all 50 states and 21 countries. Available on canvas, metal, acrylic, framed prints, and more — direct from Lima, Ohio to your wall.
