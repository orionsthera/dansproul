About Dan Sproul

Nature artwork with atmosphere, place, and a quiet sense of connection.

Dan Sproul is a fine art photographer based in Lima, Ohio, creating atmospheric landscape, wildlife, coastal, waterfall, and nature artwork for homes, collectors, and interior designers.

His work explores quiet natural places across the United States — from Ohio, the Great Lakes, and Appalachians to Alaska, the Rockies, the Pacific Coast, and beyond. The goal is simple: artwork that brings calm, warmth, and a stronger connection to place into the rooms people live in.

Practical buying help

Best formats and sizes for real interiors.

Use these starting points when choosing wall art for a home, office, gift, or larger interior project.

Best for

Bedrooms, living rooms, dining rooms, home offices, cabins, lake houses, coastal homes, wellness rooms, and quiet residential spaces.

Suggested sizes

24×36 for smaller walls, 30×40 or 32×48 for main rooms, and 40×60 or larger for statement walls, great rooms, bedrooms, and open interiors.

Strongest formats

Framed paper for refined interiors, canvas for warmth, metal for durability and modern spaces, and acrylic for polished contemporary installations.
