Award-winning photographer
based in Lima, Ohio.

Dan Sproul has spent over a decade chasing light across America's most spectacular wild places — from the glacier lakes of Alaska to the sea stacks of the Oregon Coast, the autumn peaks of Colorado to the misty hollows of the Great Smoky Mountains.

His work is sold to collectors, interior designers, hotels, and healthcare facilities worldwide. Installations include Drury Inn hotels and the Wexner Medical Center at Ohio State University.

Every image is available as a museum-quality print on canvas, metal, acrylic, or framed paper — in any size from 8×10 to 60×40 and beyond.
