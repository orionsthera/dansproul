Practical buying help

Best formats and sizes for real interiors.

Use these starting points when choosing wall art for a room, project, or commercial space.

Best for

Bedrooms, living rooms, offices, waiting rooms, hospitality lobbies, wellness spaces, cabins, lodge interiors, and quiet residential rooms.

Suggested sizes

24×36 for smaller walls, 30×40 or 32×48 for main rooms, and 40×60 or larger for statement walls, lobbies, corridors, and commercial spaces.

Strongest formats

Framed paper for refined interiors, canvas for warmth, metal for durability and modern spaces, and acrylic for polished contemporary installations.
