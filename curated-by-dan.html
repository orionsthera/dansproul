A Personal Edit

A smaller selection, chosen deliberately.

I have thousands of photographs, but more choice is not always more helpful. I selected these 24 because they represent the atmosphere, places, light, and visual character I most often want my finished work to carry into a room.

If one of these directions feels right, you can open the print page, explore the related collection, use the Art Advisor for personalized recommendations, or contact me directly. Designers can also use this page as a quick visual starting point before I search the broader 8,500+ image archive for a project.
