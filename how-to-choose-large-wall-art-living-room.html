How to Choose Large Wall Art for Living Rooms

Choosing large wall art for a living room can completely transform a space, but getting the scale, mood, and placement right makes all the difference.

Why Large Wall Art Matters in Living Rooms

Living rooms are often the largest spaces in a home, which means smaller artwork can feel lost. Large-scale artwork creates a focal point, anchors the room, and brings balance to the space.

Start with Scale and Proportion

The most common mistake is choosing artwork that is too small. A good rule is that artwork above a sofa should be about two-thirds to three-quarters the width of the furniture below it.

For larger walls, a single oversized piece often feels more intentional than multiple smaller pieces.

Consider the Mood of the Space

Before choosing artwork, think about how you want the room to feel. Calm, atmospheric scenes create a relaxed environment, while more dramatic landscapes add depth and energy.

Collections like Quiet Earth and Wellness Waters work especially well in living rooms designed for comfort and relaxation.

Placement and Height

Artwork should typically be centered at eye level, around 57–60 inches from the floor to the center. When placing art above a sofa, leave 6–10 inches between the furniture and the frame.

Framing and Presentation

Framing plays a major role in how artwork feels in a space. Natural wood frames feel warm and residential, while darker frames create a more structured, modern look.

Using Artwork to Tie a Room Together

Large wall art works best when it complements the overall palette of the room. The goal is not to match exactly, but to create a cohesive feeling through tone, texture, and atmosphere.
