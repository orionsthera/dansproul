Artwork Curation for Designers

A simpler way to source artwork for a project.

Instead of searching through thousands of images, send the room or project direction and I will narrow the artwork to a focused group that fits the space, palette, scale, and atmosphere.

1

Send the space

Share a room photo or rendering, finish palette, approximate wall dimensions, and project direction. A floorplan or inspiration image is helpful but not required.

2

Receive a focused shortlist

I will search my 8,500+ image archive and curate a small group of individual works, coordinated pairs, or artwork families that fit the project rather than sending a large general portfolio.

3

Review scale, finish & mockups

Receive recommendations for size, crop, framing or print finish, with realistic room mockups when helpful. Color and tonal adjustments can also be discussed for the project.

Print Formats, Sizing & Licensing Framed, canvas, metal, acrylic, and digital files
Recommended formats

Framed paper for refined interiors, canvas for warmth, metal for durable modern spaces, and acrylic for polished contemporary installations.

Common project sizes

24×36 for smaller walls, 30×40 or 32×48 for main rooms, and 40×60 or larger for statement walls, lobbies, corridors, and hospitality spaces.

Digital licensing

Digital licensing is available for projects that require local print production, custom fabrication, regional installers, or multi-site artwork programs.
