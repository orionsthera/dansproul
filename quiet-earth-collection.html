About the Artist

Original artwork selected for atmosphere, interior use, and emotional fit.

Dan Sproul is an Ohio-based fine art photographer and mixed media artist creating original artwork for hospitality, healthcare, corporate, wellness, and residential interiors across the United States.

His collections are organized by how a space should feel: quiet, restorative, atmospheric, grounded, memorable, or refined. That makes each page easier to use as a design resource instead of a traditional photography portfolio.

Calm, Scale & Natural Texture

Landscape photography chosen for the way it feels in a room.

Quiet Earth is a curated collection of atmospheric landscape photography created for interiors that need calm, depth, and a grounded connection to nature. The collection includes mountain reflections, quiet lakes, foggy roads, forest edges, coastal Alaska scenes, and earth-toned landscapes selected for their ability to soften modern spaces.

Rather than organizing artwork only by location, this collection is built around mood and interior use. These fine art landscape prints work especially well in hospitality suites, wellness environments, waiting rooms, residential bedrooms, living rooms, mountain homes, and quiet commercial spaces where artwork needs to create a sense of ease.

The palette leans into blue-gray atmosphere, soft greens, muted earth tones, reflective water, and natural texture. Many of the images are strong enough for oversized framed statement pieces while still remaining peaceful enough for restorative interiors.

Fine Art Landscape Prints

Artwork for interiors that need calm, atmosphere, and natural depth.

The Quiet Earth Collection brings together fine art landscape photography selected for calming interior design projects. These images are not simply grouped by location; they are curated around stillness, reflection, soft weather, mountain atmosphere, forest texture, and natural color palettes that work beautifully in modern interiors.

For interior designers, landscape wall art often needs to do more than fill an empty wall. It needs to support the mood of the room, complement the palette, and feel intentional within the architecture. Quiet Earth was created for that purpose, with images suited for large framed prints in living rooms, bedrooms, hospitality suites, wellness spaces, office lounges, and quiet commercial environments.

The collection includes atmospheric Alaska photography, mountain lake reflections, misty forest scenes, soft pastoral imagery, Badlands earth tones, and dramatic natural landscapes with a refined visual presence. These works pair especially well with natural oak, walnut, plaster, linen, stone, leather, warm neutrals, and contemporary quiet-luxury interiors.

Whether used as oversized artwork above a sofa, a calming bedroom print, a hospitality lobby focal point, or a series of coordinated landscape prints for a corridor, Quiet Earth offers a more curated approach to nature photography for interiors. The goal is simple: artwork that helps a space feel grounded, calm, and connected to the outdoors.

Fine Art Photography FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Quiet Earth artwork.

What interiors work best with Quiet Earth?

Quiet Earth was created for calm contemporary interiors, wellness-inspired spaces, mountain homes, boutique hospitality projects, minimalist residential interiors, and refined natural design aesthetics.

Are these images available as oversized framed prints?

Yes. Many Quiet Earth photographs work especially well as oversized statement artwork above beds, fireplaces, dining room consoles, office walls, and hospitality focal walls.

Can designers request recommendations?

Absolutely. Interior designers can email directly for coordinated artwork suggestions, sizing guidance, or help selecting multiple pieces for residential or commercial projects.

Why organize by atmosphere instead of location?

Quiet Earth is curated around mood, tone, palette, and interior compatibility. This creates a more useful experience for designers and homeowners searching for artwork that fits the feeling of a room.
