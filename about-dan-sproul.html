Meet Dan

A lifelong connection to landscape and place.

Dan Sproul is a fine art photographer and mixed media artist based in Lima, Ohio. His work is driven by a fascination with atmosphere, place, and the emotional connection people feel to landscapes.

Over the years he has photographed throughout the United States, from Alaska and the Rocky Mountains to the Pacific Coast, Great Lakes, Appalachian forests, waterfalls, wildlife habitats, and quiet rural landscapes.

The goal is simple: create artwork that helps people bring a sense of calm, memory, and connection to nature into the spaces where they live and work.
