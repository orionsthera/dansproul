Collection Story

Where Sporting Heritage Meets Fine Art

The Heritage Sporting Collection blends vintage patent artwork with layered outdoor imagery inspired by hunting, fishing, wildlife, and classic sporting traditions. These pieces are not simple patent drawings — they are mixed-media artworks designed to feel nostalgic, collected, and visually suited for refined lodge interiors.



From duck calls and decoys to hunting knives, sporting rifles, archery equipment, camper designs, and wilderness tools, the collection creates a cohesive visual world for cabins, lake homes, lodges, boutique hospitality spaces, and masculine executive interiors.
