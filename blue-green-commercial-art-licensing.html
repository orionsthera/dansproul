Plain-language license summary

What the standard license does — and does not — cover.

This summary keeps the buying decision simple. Final licensing details can be confirmed in writing with the project invoice or license agreement.

Included

  • Interior display at one named commercial location.
  • Up to twelve physical reproductions across the eight supplied images.
  • Reasonable cropping, resizing and print-process color correction.
  • File transfer to the contracted printer or fabricator solely for this project.
  • Non-exclusive use; copyright remains with Dan Sproul Photography.

Requires separate permission

  • Additional properties, locations or projects.
  • More than twelve physical reproductions.
  • Advertising, websites, social media, packaging or promotional campaigns.
  • Resale, merchandise, stock distribution, sublicensing or file sharing outside the project team.
  • AI training, dataset use, model training, NFTs or derivative commercial products.
Licensing FAQ

Common project questions.

Can my printer crop the images to fit standard sizes?

Yes. Reasonable cropping and resizing for the licensed installation are permitted. Major artistic alterations should be approved first.

Can a designer purchase the license for a client?

Yes. The project location and end client should be identified in the license. The designer and contracted production vendors may handle the files only as needed to complete that licensed project.

Can the same image be printed more than once?

Yes. Individual works may be repeated as long as the total number of physical reproductions does not exceed twelve at the licensed location.

Can the files be used at multiple properties?

The standard license covers one named location. Multi-site and additional-location licenses can be quoted separately.

Are printing and framing included?

No. This product licenses the digital artwork for local production. Printing, framing, fabrication and installation are handled separately.
